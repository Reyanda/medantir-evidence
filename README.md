## 🌟 Overview

Medantir Evidence is an autonomous, publication-grade evidence synthesis and systematic review workbench. It conducts PRISMA-compliant literature screening, Cochrane Risk of Bias (RoB 2 / ROBINS-I), GRADE certainty scoring, meta-analysis, and causal directed acyclic graph (DAG) validation.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Automated Title & Abstract Screening**: Dual-reviewer simulation with Cohen's Kappa calibration and conflict resolution.
- **Full-Text PDF Extraction & Data Charting**: Extracts quantitative effect estimates (OR, RR, HR, MD, SMD) and baseline sample characteristics.
- **Risk of Bias Assessments**: Standardized domain assessments following Cochrane RoB 2 (RCTs) and ROBINS-I (observational studies).
- **GRADE Evidence Profile Generator**: Certainty of evidence ratings across risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, and publication bias.
- **Causal Evidence Mapping**: MECAST and bias triangulation with causal structure graphs.
